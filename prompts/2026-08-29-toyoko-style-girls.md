## Prompt 1（縁石・ツインテール・黒×ピンク地雷系）

A photorealistic night street portrait of a young adult Japanese woman in her early
twenties with long black twin tails, sitting on a curb in a neon-lit Kabukicho back
street, looking at her phone with a tired, distant expression. She wears a black and
pink jirai-kei outfit with lace trim, a ribbon choker, and chunky platform boots.
Pink and blue neon signs glow in the blurred background. Moody available-light
photography, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, vertical composition.
